# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Bank and credit union executives, CMOs, CFOs, and growth leaders evaluating marketing measurement, branch markets, acquisition footprints, or fractional marketing leadership. They read on a laptop between meetings or on a phone from a LinkedIn link, and need to understand the decision, the inputs required, and the concrete output quickly enough to decide whether a conversation is worthwhile. Many work in regulated institutions and read with a compliance officer's eye.

## Product Purpose
pauldrago.com is the advisory practice of Paul Drago (operated through Glass Vase, LLC). It earns a first conversation: articles show how Paul thinks, service pages show what he delivers, and every page keeps booking a call one step away. Success is a qualified executive booking a 20-minute call, or returning to read the next article.

## Brand Personality
Direct, financially credible, quietly confident. The writing sounds like an experienced operator speaking plainly to another executive, without agency language, inflated claims, or generic AI phrasing. Pages should leave the reader feeling they have been handed a well-prepared board document: composed, evidenced, and respectful of their time.

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing-page templates: soft cards, excessive rounding, gradients, hero metrics, decorative dashboards.
- Generic entrance animations and scroll gimmicks that delay reading.
- Stock-consulting imagery (handshakes, skylines, people pointing at screens).
- Legal-wall privacy and terms pages written in boilerplate.

## Design Principles
1. Lead with the decision the institution needs to make.
2. Use diagrams and models only when they clarify the work or its output.
3. Keep navigation and next steps visible, especially between the services overview and product pages.
4. Favor readable hierarchy and evidence over marketing volume.
5. Preserve a composed, executive tone across desktop and mobile, and practice what the site preaches: fast pages (PageSpeed 100 on mobile) and honest data handling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at least 4.5:1 contrast in both light and dark themes; visible focus states; full keyboard operation; reduced-motion alternatives for any animation; no information carried by colour alone.
