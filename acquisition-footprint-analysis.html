Primary public-data sources

  1. FDIC Summary of Deposits for branch deposits, deposit market share, and market-presence reporting.
  2. FDIC Data Tools for institution history, mergers, branch structure, and financial reports.
  3. NCUA Call Report data for federally insured credit-union financial and institutional information.
  4. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey for demographic, household, housing, and economic context.
