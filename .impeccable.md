## Design Context

### Users
Bank and credit union executives, CMOs, CFOs, and growth leaders evaluating marketing measurement, branch markets, acquisition footprints, or fractional marketing leadership. They need to understand the decision, the inputs required, and the concrete output quickly enough to decide whether a conversation is worthwhile.

### Brand Personality
Direct, financially credible, and quietly confident. The writing should sound like an experienced operator speaking plainly to another executive, without agency language, inflated claims, or generic AI phrasing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial financial-services advisory: a pale cool ground (#F1F2F4) with navy ink (#0A192F) and one terracotta accent (#D9552B), hard rules, dense but orderly information, and restrained analytical graphics. Dark mode follows the browser preference and inverts to navy ground, paper ink and a lifted terracotta (#E8734A); both themes share one set of token names in site-shell.css. Type: Archivo Black for display, Source Serif 4 for long reading, IBM Plex Sans for instruments. The interface should feel closer to a well-prepared board document than a SaaS landing-page template. Avoid soft cards, excessive rounding, gradients, decorative dashboards, generic entrance animations, and stock-consulting imagery.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the decision the institution needs to make.
2. Use diagrams and models only when they clarify the work or its output.
3. Keep navigation and next steps visible, especially between the services overview and product pages.
4. Favor readable hierarchy and evidence over marketing volume.
5. Preserve a composed, executive tone across desktop and mobile.
